Overview

The game story A journey of innocence began with a curious yet naïve thirst for success…a longing for the next chapter in life shared by your girlfriend Nikki and your best friend Mini. What started as ambition soon spiraled into temptation, as both women stepped onto a path of seduction and betrayal. Witness the unfolding of their NTR story, where your own missteps blind you to the shifting tides in your relationship. Powerless to stop it, you watch as your love is stolen, twisted, and ultimately corrupted by the allure of lust and the intoxicating glow of fame. Will you save love… before it’s too late or just be a witness.
