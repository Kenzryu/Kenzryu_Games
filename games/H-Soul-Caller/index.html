Overview

The game story a boy abandoned at a young age and raised in the criminal underworld by a gang member, eventually becoming one of the organization’s most trusted enforcers. The game begins after MC leaves his violent past behind in search of a new life. He now lives with Ren, a kind-hearted and gentle girl who, like him, was abandoned by her father. As he settles into his new routine, he attends college, befriends the cheerful Iroha, Anastasia and cold lecturer Koharu, works part-time at a cozy café run by the lazy owner, Nora. But MC’s peaceful days are short-lived. Mysterious evil spirits begin to emerge, forcing him into battle. To confront this threat, he must become a Soul Caller, who can summon the souls of girls to fight alongside him. He joins the Soul Caller organization under the supervision of Pandora Life is about more than building relationships with a cast of beautiful heroines. MC must also face dangerous supernatural forces in spectacular, action-packed battles that will determine the fate of both his new life and the world around him.
