Overview

The story game Join Will and his girlfriend, April, as they navigate their way through college. Motivated by lust, love, and ambition, the couple and their friends will test boundaries with themselves and the world around them. If Will and April's relationship wasn't complicated enough with them being in college, how much more messy will it get with parties, social media, money, and reputations thrown in the mix? Not to mention, a few fantasy races roam the world of Brain Rot. Watch April and Will interact with elves, orcs, minotaurs, and who knows what else. The couple will do their best as they balance school and relationships as they try to uncover the mystery of how these fantastical beings came to earth in the first place.
