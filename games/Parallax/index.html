Overview

The game story in the near future, power doesn’t live in the open. It lives in quiet rooms, deniable operations, and people who can be erased. You are one of those people. Good at the job. Loyal to your squad. Pragmatic about who signs the cheques. Just another professional in a world that runs on people like you. And then everything changes. Parallax is a near-future conspiracy thriller and adult visual novel about identity, control, and the relationships that form in the spaces between missions. The tone aims to be colder and more grounded than most games in the genre, focused on consequence, shifting loyalties, and what people become when they are used as tools.
