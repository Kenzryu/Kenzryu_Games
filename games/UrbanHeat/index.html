The game story After a broken heart, you searched for peace in Sunshore, a seaside village where time seems to stand still. But behind the friendly faces and pretty smiles, you find a world of hidden desires, strange favors, and neighbors who can’t keep their hands to themselves. How far will you go to fit in? What doors will open when no one is looking? In Sunshore, the heat doesn’t just come from the sun, and your fresh start will be anything but relaxed.
