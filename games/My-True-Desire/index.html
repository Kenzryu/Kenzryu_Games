Overview

My True Desire is a story that follows the journey of a young man deeply in love, as he uncovers hidden desires he never knew he had. Passion, fear, excitement, love, anxiety… how will he navigate these overwhelming emotions? And now that he’s allowing himself to explore these newfound feelings, how will his relationship with Ana evolve? Who will they become? The game focuses on the main character’s desires to show off and share his girlfriend, with a strong emphasis on the main character’s perspective. While your choices won’t alter the overall storyline, they will influence a few scenes and dialogues you get to experience.​
