Overview

The story game follows the lives of a married couple: Andy, an executive at a major firm, and Sarah, a dedicated teacher. Their world is upended when Andy is forced to relocate out of town for work. The distance becomes the ultimate crucible, pushing the dynamics of their marriage to the absolute limit. Which path will they ultimately choose?
