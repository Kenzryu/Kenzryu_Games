Overview

The story game Glory of Holly is a visual novel in the Female Protagonist genre, where the player guides a young woman’s journey from a “hard-working, ambitious honors student” to someone discovering new sides of life. Holly White moves out from her parents’ home and must find a job to pay rent and prove her independence. Circumstances lead her to a large media company with a rather unusual field, and that’s where her first serious choices begin. The game features a point system: Good (the path of a kind, responsible girl focused on love and stability) and Bad (the path of temptation, ambition, and risky experiments). Every choice matters: Holly may remain faithful to herself and her boyfriend, or she may be swayed by new acquaintances pulling her in a very different direction. Each decision changes the story, relationships, and the scenes you can experience. Expect realistic dialogues, dramatic conflicts, romance, friendship, and moral dilemmas. With updates released on the 1st of every month, the story will expand, and Holly’s fate will depend entirely on the player.​
