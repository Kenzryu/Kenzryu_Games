Overview

The Story Games As the name implies, this is another wife and mother game with a distinct theme. You take on the character of Christine Watson, a happily married middle-aged lady. Her spouse has found a new job after losing his previous one. Christine has an 18-year-old son and a beautiful daughter. Christine has always longed for an exciting life. However, due to financial constraints, she may need to discover alternative ways to make life more adventurous. Life is full of sadness, loneliness, and suffering and ends too soon. Your choices determine Christine's path. Excitement or loneliness. In reality, it's the path you would take for yourself.
