Overview

The game story you play as the protagonist whose family has mysteriously gone missing, leaving you alone to manage the apartment building they once owned. Not long after, several girls from another world suddenly appear in your life. With nowhere else to go, you take them in and promise to help them find a way back to their world while also searching for clues about your missing family. To support yourselves, you reopen the café on the ground floor, earning money, paying off debts, and helping the girls adjust to life in this unfamiliar world. Your once quiet life is about to become far more chaotic than you ever imagined.
