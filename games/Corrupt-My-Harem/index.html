Overview

The story game young man who becomes bound to a powerful demon, gaining dark magic that lets him twist emotions and lure others toward his side. With this corrupting power, he sets out to build a harem by influencing, tempting, and reshaping the desires of the women he encounters. As the demon's influence grows, he must decide whether he's becoming the master of this power—or merely its pawn.​
